# Shared Musical Theme

The recurring theme is an original eight-note phrase in **D Dorian**:

`D4 – F4 – A4 – G4 | E4 – F4 – B4 – A4`

Rhythm in beats:

`1 – 1/2 – 1 1/2 – 1 | 1 – 1/2 – 1/2 – 2`

The natural B gives the minor/modal material a small unresolved lift. That lets
the same phrase feel curious in the daytime, lonely at night, and quietly tense
in the danger cue.

Track treatments:

- **Main menu:** exposed bell version, slow and spacious.
- **Forest day:** one octave higher, plucked and supported by light percussion.
- **Forest night:** lower register, reduced to a fragment with long silences.
- **Discovery:** brighter, bell-like statement.
- **Quiet danger:** low fragment over altered chords and a pulse.
- **Credits:** fuller flute statement that resolves more gently.

The MIDI files can be edited in most DAWs and notation applications.
