FoxDot Asset Pack Manifest
==========================

Two bundled packs: visual (models/textures, CC0) and audio (music/ambience/
sfx, see LICENSE.txt for its distinct terms). audio/manifest.json is a
separate, structured manifest consumed by internal/audio/catalog.go at
runtime (durations, BPM, loop flags, ...) — this file is just the flat
human-readable file listing.
